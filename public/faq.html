How can we help you?

Find quick answers to the most common questions about your order, shipping, and our service.

Order & Payment

Once your payment is approved, you'll be redirected to your personal order portal where you can see a green "Order Confirmed" status. You'll also receive a confirmation email with your order details and a direct link to this page.
We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover), as well as PayPal and Apple Pay. All transactions are processed securely with 256-bit SSL encryption.
Absolutely. We use industry-standard SSL encryption and do not store your credit card details on our servers. Your payment is processed through a PCI-DSS compliant payment gateway, ensuring maximum security for all transactions.
Orders can be modified or cancelled within 2 hours of purchase, as long as they haven't entered the processing stage. Please contact our support team immediately via the live chat if you need to make changes.

Shipping & Delivery

Standard shipping takes 5-8 business days to the United States, Canada, United Kingdom, Australia, and New Zealand. Your order will be dispatched within 48 business hours after payment confirmation. Tracking information will be updated as your package moves through the delivery network.
Yes! We currently ship to the United States, Canada, United Kingdom, Australia, and New Zealand. Shipping is free for all orders. Delivery times may vary slightly depending on your location and local customs processing.
You can track your order directly on the main page of your order portal. Simply enter your tracking number in the "Track Your Order" section. Once your package is shipped, you'll receive a tracking code via email that you can use to monitor delivery status in real time.
Delays can occasionally occur due to high demand, customs processing, or carrier issues. If your package hasn't arrived within the estimated timeframe, please contact our support team and we'll investigate immediately. We'll keep you updated every step of the way.
In most cases, no additional fees apply. However, depending on your country's import regulations, customs duties or taxes may be charged upon delivery. These fees are determined by your local customs authority and are the responsibility of the recipient.

The Mystery Box

The Exclusive Mystery Box is a curated package containing a surprise selection of brand-name products. Each box is carefully hand-picked and the contents are valued at significantly more than the purchase price. It's a fun and exciting way to discover new products!
Mystery Boxes may include a wide variety of items such as electronics, gadgets, accessories, lifestyle products, and more. Each box is unique and the specific contents are a surprise — that's what makes it exciting! All items are brand new and in original packaging.
Yes! Every Mystery Box contains products with a combined retail value that exceeds the $29 purchase price. Many customers have reported receiving items valued at 3-5x the price they paid. The exact contents vary with each box.
Due to the nature of mystery boxes, we cannot accept returns or exchanges for the contents. However, if your box arrives damaged or if any items are defective, please contact our support team within 7 days of delivery and we'll arrange a replacement.
