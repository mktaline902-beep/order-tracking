Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 9, 2026

Your Order Portal ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the rights you have over your data.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you voluntarily provide when placing an order or contacting us:

  • Full name and shipping address
  • Email address and phone number
  • Payment information (processed securely by our payment partner — we never store your full card details)

We also automatically collect technical data such as IP address, browser type, device, and pages visited, in order to operate and improve the service.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used to:

  • Process and fulfill your order
  • Provide customer support and respond to your questions

  • Improve our website and service quality

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We share information only with:

  • Payment processors — to process your transaction securely

4. Data Retention

We keep your personal data only as long as necessary to provide the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. Order records are typically retained for 5 years for tax and accounting purposes.

5. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (GDPR, CCPA, etc.), you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Receive a copy of your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@you-order-portal.xyz.

6. Security

We use industry-standard security measures including TLS/SSL encryption, secure payment gateways, and access controls to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's Privacy

Our service is not directed to anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can delete it.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this section reflects the most recent revision. Material changes will be communicated via email or a prominent notice on the website.

Refund Policy

Last updated: April 9, 2026

We stand behind our product and want every customer to be satisfied. This Refund Policy explains when refunds are available and how to request one.

1. Eligibility for Refunds

You may request a full refund if:

  • Your order has not yet been shipped (refund within 24 hours)
  • Your package was lost in transit and was not delivered after a reasonable investigation period
  • Your order arrived physically damaged or defective
  • You received an item that is significantly different from what was advertised

2. Non-Refundable Situations

Due to the nature of mystery boxes, we cannot offer refunds in the following cases:

  • Dissatisfaction with the specific items received (the surprise factor is part of the product)
  • Change of mind after the order has been shipped
  • Items damaged after delivery due to misuse

3. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, please use our refund request form or contact our support team. You'll need to provide:

  • Your order email address
  • The reason for your refund request
  • Photos of damaged items (if applicable)

4. Processing Time

Once your refund request is approved, the amount will be credited to your original payment method within 5–10 business days. Depending on your bank or card issuer, it may take additional time to appear in your account.

5. Partial Refunds

In some cases (e.g., partial damage to a box) we may offer a partial refund instead of a full one, subject to mutual agreement.

6. Contact

For any refund-related question, please email refunds@you-order-portal.xyz.

Shipping Policy

Last updated: April 9, 2026

1. Where We Ship

We currently ship to the following countries:

  • United States
  • Canada
  • United Kingdom
  • Australia
  • New Zealand

2. Processing Time

Orders are processed and dispatched within 1–3 business days after payment confirmation. During promotional periods or holidays, processing may take an additional 1–2 days.

3. Delivery Times

Estimated delivery times after dispatch:

  • United States: 5–8 business days
  • Canada: 7–12 business days
  • United Kingdom: 7–14 business days
  • Australia & New Zealand: 10–18 business days

Delivery times are estimates and may vary depending on customs processing, local carriers, and weather conditions.

4. Shipping Costs

Standard shipping is free on all orders. There are no hidden fees at checkout.

5. Tracking

Once your order is dispatched, you'll receive a tracking code by email. You can also track your order directly on our website using the tracking section on the home page.

6. Customs & Import Duties

For international orders, additional customs duties or import taxes may be charged by your local authorities upon delivery. These fees are determined by your country's customs office and are the responsibility of the recipient. Your Order Portal is not responsible for any such charges.

7. Lost or Delayed Packages

If your package is significantly delayed (more than 30 days from dispatch with no tracking updates), please contact our support team. We will open an investigation with the carrier and, if the package cannot be located, we will issue a full refund or send a replacement.

8. Incorrect Address

Please double-check your shipping address before placing the order. We are not responsible for packages sent to incorrect addresses provided by the customer. If you notice an error, contact us within 2 hours of placing the order so we can attempt to correct it before dispatch.

Last updated: April 9, 2026

This website uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to improve your experience, analyze usage, and deliver relevant content.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They allow the website to recognize your browser and remember information such as preferences and login state.

2. Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential cookies — required for the website to function (e.g., session, security). These cannot be disabled.
  • Functional cookies — remember your preferences (e.g., language, region).
  • Analytics cookies — help us understand how visitors interact with the website (anonymized statistics).
  • Marketing cookies — used by advertising partners to show you relevant ads. Only set with your consent.

3. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies are set by third-party services we use, such as the live chat widget and the order tracking widget. We do not control these cookies — please refer to the relevant third party's policy for more information.

4. Managing Cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling essential cookies may affect the functionality of the website.

5. Updates

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Please review it periodically to stay informed about our use of cookies.
